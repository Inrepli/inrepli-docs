## Data Schema

Inrepli organizes platform data into objects. Each object represents a distinct type of information, such as a Contact, Company, Conversation, or Meeting.

These objects are connected to provide a complete view of each inbound relationship and the actions taken by your Agent and team.

## Core Objects

| Object             | Description                                                                        |
| ------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Contacts**       | Individual people who interact with your business through connected Channels.      |
| **Companies**      | Business organizations associated with one or more Contacts.                       |
| **Conversations**  | Interactions between Contacts, Agents, and team members across connected Channels. |
| **Meetings**       | Scheduled conversations between a Contact and an Account Manager.                  |
| **Users**          | People who have access to the Inrepli platform, including Account Managers.        |
| **Forms**          | Structured information collected from Contacts to create or update records.        |
| **Knowledge Base** | The information your Agent uses to answer Contact questions.                       |

### Contacts

Contacts are individual people who interact with your business. A Contact may be a potential customer, an existing customer, or another person communicating through a connected Channel.

A Contact record can include identity details, Contact information, Qualification and Verification results, Conversations, Meetings, and associated Companies.

### Companies

Companies are business organizations associated with Contacts.

A Company can have one or more related Contacts. The Company record stores shared information such as industry, size, location, website, and enriched business data.

### Conversations

Conversations contain interactions between Contacts, Agents, and team members across your connected Channels.

A Conversation may include messages from web chat, email, WhatsApp, or other supported Channels. Conversations are associated with the relevant Contact and, when known, their Company.

### Meetings

Meetings are scheduled Conversations between a Contact and an Account Manager.

Meeting records can include the participants, scheduled time, meeting status, related Conversation, and assigned Account Manager.

### Users

Users are people who have access to the Inrepli platform.

Users may have different roles and permissions. Account Managers are Users who can receive assigned Contacts, manage Conversations, and participate in Meetings.

### Forms

Forms collect structured information from Contacts.

Form submissions can create or update Contact and Company records. The information collected may also support Qualification, Verification, Assignment, and Scheduling.

### Knowledge Base

The Knowledge Base contains the information your Agent uses to answer Contact questions.

The Knowledge Base may include websites, uploaded documents, public links, and approved FAQs. Together, these sources form the Agent's searchable knowledge.

## How the Objects Work Together

A Contact can be associated with a Company and participate in multiple Conversations and Meetings. Conversations collect the Contact's interactions, while Meetings record scheduled discussions with Account Managers.

Forms, Data Enrichment, and Agent activity can add or update information within these records. The Knowledge Base provides the information the Agent uses when communicating with Contacts.

## Coming Soon

### Agents

Inrepli currently supports one inbound Agent for each Customer account.

Future support for multiple Agents will allow Customers to create Agents for different teams, brands, regions, Channels, or business functions.

### Campaigns

Campaigns will organize a planned series of interactions with Contacts.

A Campaign may include messages, content, target audiences, Channels, and scheduled actions. Campaign activity will connect with existing Contact, Company, and Conversation records.
