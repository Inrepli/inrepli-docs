## Conversations

A Conversation is created when Inrepli receives an inbound message through a connected Channel and the Agent responds.

Conversations store the full exchange between a Contact, the Agent, and any team members who join the interaction. A Contact can have multiple Conversations across different Channels, and each Conversation remains connected to the same Contact record when matching Contact information is available.

## Spam indicators

When a conversation is marked as Spam, its Spam indicator can show the recorded reason and confidence when available. Open the indicator in the conversation header to review those details.

Users with permission to update the conversation can choose **Not Spam** and confirm to remove its Spam status. A bot or automated-traffic identity is separate from the Spam decision; review the Spam indicator rather than assuming that every automated visitor is Spam.

## Conversation Archiving

Inrepli automatically archives inactive Conversations after a set period. The archive period depends on the Channel and begins after the Contact's most recent message.

| Channel  | Conversation archived after |
| -------- | --------------------------- |
| Web chat | 30 minutes                  |
| WhatsApp | 24 hours                    |
| Email    | 7 days                      |

Archiving closes the active Conversation but does not delete its messages or remove it from the Contact's history. Archived Conversations remain available for review.

A new inbound message after archiving will begin a new Conversation. Earlier messages remain in the Contact's history for your team to review.

## Continue on Another Channel

Temporary Channels, such as web chat, may no longer be available after a Contact leaves your website or closes the chat window.

When another verified Contact method is available, the Agent can continue communicating through a different connected Channel. For example, the Agent may continue by email after a web chat session ends.

The new messages remain associated with the Contact, allowing your team to follow the interaction across Channels.

## Conversation Summaries

Inrepli creates a Conversation Summary 30 minutes after the last message from the Contact.

The delay allows the Agent to include messages that may arrive shortly after the initial exchange. The summary provides a concise overview of the Contact's request, the information collected, the actions taken, and any next steps.

Conversation Summaries help Account Managers and other Users understand the interaction without reviewing every message.

## Website visit context

Website Conversations can show the current visitor's traffic classification and available source-verification details. A suspected AI-agent or crawler identity is distinguished from a verified source; bot identity and Spam are separate assessments.

The Contact profile beside a Conversation can also show recorded website activity and visit summaries when Webtracking and the required consent are available. See [Contact website activity](/data/contacts#website-activity).

## Resources shared in replies

Website Conversation history keeps resources shared with an Agent reply alongside the message, including relevant Training files, media and links. If an optional resource cannot be verified, the text answer can still be delivered without it.

## Conversation Usage

Inrepli measures usage by Agent Conversations rather than by tokens.

Each Inrepli plan includes a monthly Conversation allowance. A Conversation counts toward usage when the Agent responds to an inbound Contact.

When the included monthly allowance is reached, Inrepli automatically adds another Conversation package. Conversations included in a top-up package can be used during the month they were purchased and the following month.

Your workspace usage shows the number of Conversations used, the remaining allowance, and any additional Conversation packages that have been added.
