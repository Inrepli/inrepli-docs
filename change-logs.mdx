## September 24, 2026

### Choose when Contacts receive meeting reminders

Set Contact email reminders for one day, one hour, or fifteen minutes before a meeting in Workspace Meeting Scheduling. These controls are separate from each Account Manager's personal reminders. [Scheduling](/inbound-agent/scheduling#contact-email-reminders)

### Open the right page from Assistant

Ask the Assistant to open a permitted App page and use supported Contacts or Companies search filters. The conversation keeps a record of the navigation. [Support Assistant](/mcp/support#open-app-pages-from-support)

### Keep website Training easier to maintain

Workspaces with expanded website crawling enabled can follow page discovery and processing, inspect failed or excluded pages, and resume saved work within their configured limit. Knowledge views also render Markdown tables more clearly, and document uploads handle filenames more reliably. [Training](/inbound-agent/training#follow-website-update-progress)

### Manage Qualification and Routing in your Agent

Qualification Settings brings the qualification method and Buyer Readiness setup together. Required/Optional settings can be adjusted after answers exist without changing protected question definitions. Routing settings, Account Managers, and history now sit under the Agent. [Qualification](/inbound-agent/qualification#maintain-buyer-readiness-questions) · [Routing](/inbound-agent/routing#find-routing-settings)

### Keep Contact and Company records connected

Search and select an existing Company from a Contact, or create one there when your permissions allow. Review Contact tags within Qualification. [Contacts](/data/contacts)

### Read website activity and campaign sources more clearly

Website activity groups events by page while retaining the detailed event list. Original and latest attribution preserve available campaign details, including UTM content, term, and campaign ID, without mixing in Playground simulations. [Website SDK](/connections/website-sdk#website-behavior-tracking)

### Prepare for WhatsApp connection

The WhatsApp setup guide now lays out the business account, phone verification, and business identity information to prepare. The Playground toolbar also keeps Platform, Inbox, and Contact Profile controls usable on smaller screens. [WhatsApp](/connections/whatsapp#connect-whatsapp)

### Query Contacts with clearer API and MCP filters

Filter Contacts by their original creation channel, use explicit timezones in timestamp filters, and request exact totals when needed. MCP guidance makes owner counts and team-member name matching more precise. [API filters](/api-reference/filtering-pagination#value-semantics)
