## October 30, 2026

### More useful Support and MCP answers

Ask for conversation history, meeting details and unfinished tasks from the records you can access. Open the corresponding Inrepli record from the result. In Support, CRM changes are proposed in a confirmation card and applied after you confirm.

### Buyer Readiness across Email and Form

Buyer Readiness processes multiple questions in Email and Form interactions and restores saved answers and refusal states across conversation segments. Information already provided remains available, while question presentation and answer persistence are tracked separately.

### More reliable conversations and website activity

Support preserves replies already in progress in their original conversation. Email messages keep their character encoding, and channel messages remain distinguishable. Webtracking summaries use recorded behavior and available page knowledge without treating every custom-control click as a completed action. Web Chat publication records also show the operation source and initiating user.
