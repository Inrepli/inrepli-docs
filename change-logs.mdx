### AI Agent Reception

Enable external AI consultations in **Website → AI Agent Reception**. Choose automatic discovery links through the Website SDK or publish the downloaded files on your website. Review external conversations in Inbox. Websites configured for AI booking can receive meeting requests after the visitor confirms a time.

### Clearer errors and meeting records

Page errors explain what happened and offer recovery where available. The meeting list and detail identify simulated meetings, and a host’s email can identify an unnamed host. Calendar disconnect confirms the action using the same wording across providers.

### HubSpot sync and Playground preparation

Choose how eligible Contact and Company updates reach HubSpot, review mappings and see individual outcomes in Sync Logs. The Playground shows localized preparation details and separates FAQ coverage from the setup needed for activation.

### Support, chat and website activity

Support retains panel and conversation state during the session and opens Web Chat settings for proposed changes. Website chat handles loading and unread reminders more consistently. Website activity shows more provider and crawler labels, and enabled email signatures can use the reply language.
