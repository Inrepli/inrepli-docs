## Meetings

Inrepli can manage scheduling and meeting booking without requiring a separate calendar, scheduling, or video conferencing platform. You can also connect the calendar and meeting tools your team already uses.

Each Meeting record stores the scheduled time, Contact, assigned Account Manager, meeting status, related Conversation, and available preparation details.

## Calendar and Meeting Integrations

Inrepli integrates with widely used calendar, scheduling, and meeting tools. These Integrations allow Inrepli to check Account Manager availability, create calendar events, and include the correct meeting details.

When a connected tool is used, meeting updates remain synchronized between Inrepli and the external platform. This helps prevent scheduling conflicts and keeps both systems current.

Account Managers can also use Inrepli's native Scheduling features when they do not want to rely on an external scheduling tool.

## Suggested Meeting Topics

Before a Meeting, the Agent creates suggested topics to help the Account Manager prepare.

Suggested topics may be based on:

- Topics requested by the Contact
- Questions raised during the Conversation
- Website activity, when available
- Contact and Company information
- Qualification and Verification results
- The Agent's assessment of the Contact's needs

These suggestions give the Account Manager a concise view of what the Contact is likely to want to discuss. They are intended as preparation guidance and can be adjusted by the Account Manager.

## Meeting Controls

Account Managers can choose how Meetings are added to their calendars.

With automatic booking, an eligible Contact can select an available time and the Meeting is added to the Account Manager's calendar.

With meeting approval, the Contact submits a request and the Account Manager reviews the Contact profile before accepting. The Meeting is added to the calendar only after approval.

With manual booking, the Account Manager receives the request and creates the Meeting themselves.

Account Managers can also require Contact authentication before booking. When authentication is required, the Contact must verify their email address before accessing available meeting times or confirming a Meeting.

These controls allow each Account Manager to balance scheduling speed with the level of review they require.

## Meeting Notifications

Inrepli sends notifications to both the Contact and the assigned Account Manager when important Meeting events occur.

Notifications may include:

- Meeting booked
- Meeting rescheduled
- Meeting cancelled
- Meeting reminders

Each notification includes the latest Meeting details. When an external calendar or meeting tool is connected, updates are also synchronized with that tool where supported.

Meeting notifications help both participants stay informed and reduce missed or outdated appointments.
