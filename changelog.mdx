## Smarter qualification and clearer customer activity

This release helps teams qualify buyers more consistently, understand website and form activity, and benefit from more reliable setup and lifecycle communications.

### More consistent buyer qualification

- Organize required and optional Budget, Authority, Need, and Timing questions and connect them to compatible Contact fields.
- Let the Agent suggest relevant questions and continue with unanswered questions during customer conversations.

### Clearer, consent-aware activity

- Respect plan availability and configured consent preferences when collecting website activity.
- See Form submissions and Website/Webchat activity more promptly across Inbox and contact views.

### More reliable setup and communications

- Tenant setup and website refresh tasks now recover more reliably and provide clearer progress and completion states.
- Playground and billing lifecycle emails are handled more consistently, including duplicate prevention and cancellation when they are no longer needed.

## Training knowledge management improvements

Find, review, and maintain your agent’s knowledge more efficiently.

### Search and history

- Search completed FAQs and document content using keyword or semantic search.
- Review changes to FAQs, websites, files, and document chunks, with filters for source and who made each change.

### Safer knowledge maintenance

- Preview which pending FAQs may be affected before deleting supporting knowledge, then choose whether to update or remove those FAQs.
- Filter FAQs by category, creator, and review status, and approve or delete multiple FAQs at once.
- Add an individual page from the same website directly to an existing website knowledge source.
