---
title: Cannot Connect CRM
description: Resolve CRM integration issues.
---

## Symptoms

- Error: "CRM connection failed"
- No leads syncing

## Solutions

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check credentials">
    Ensure your CRM API key is correct.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Re-authorize access">
    Retry the connection in Integrations > CRM.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Allow pop-ups">
    Some CRMs require pop-ups for sign-in.
  </Step>
</Steps>
