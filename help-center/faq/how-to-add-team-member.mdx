---

## Overview

You can invite teammates to collaborate in your Inrepli Docs workspace.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Team Settings">
    Log in and click "Team" in the sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invite member">
    Click "Invite Team Member" and enter their email.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send invite">
    Click "Send Invite". They'll get an invite link by email.
  </Step>
</Steps>
