---
title: Setting Up Auto-Replies
description: Enable ai-powered responses in Inrepli.
---

## Get Started

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Workflows">
    Open Inrepli Docs and click "Workflows" in the sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create Auto-Reply">
    Click "New Workflow" > "Auto-Reply".
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure trigger">
    Set conditions, e.g. "Inbound Lead".
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write reply message">
    Add your message. Use variables like `{lead_name}`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable">
    Turn workflow ON. Replies will now send instantly.
  </Step>
</Steps>
