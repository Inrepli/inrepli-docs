---
title: Support Assistant
description: Use the in-product Support Assistant to review guidance, continue previous conversations, and act on relevant workspace tasks.
---

## Get help inside Inrepli

The Support Assistant is available while you work in the Inrepli platform. Open it to ask a question, review guidance, or understand the next action for a setup or account task without leaving your current workflow.

The Assistant uses the current platform session and the signed-in User's access. It does not expose another User's private Assistant conversations.

## Continue a conversation

Your Support Assistant conversations are saved so you can close the panel and return later. Open conversation history to review an earlier exchange or start a new conversation when you want a separate topic.

When a response is still loading, wait for it to finish before switching conversations. If a response cannot be completed, the Assistant keeps the conversation available so you can retry.

## Review proactive guidance

The Support Assistant can notify you when new guidance is available. Depending on the situation, the notification may open the relevant conversation automatically or remain available from the Assistant entry until you review or dismiss it.

Proactive guidance can summarize relevant setup and account tasks. The original task remains the source of truth for its current status and action.

## Open the relevant action

When guidance refers to a task or setting, use the provided action to open the relevant Inrepli page. Review the current page state before making a change; the Support Assistant does not replace the controls and permissions of the destination page.
