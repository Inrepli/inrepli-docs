## Train your agent with the information your team already uses

Training gives your Inbound Agent the context it needs to answer questions the way your team would. The quality of that training directly affects response accuracy, consistency, and how well the agent reflects your products, policies, and brand voice in live conversations.

## Knowledge sources

Use a mix of structured and high-signal content so the agent can answer both common questions and edge cases. Start with your most current customer-facing materials, then add internal guidance that shapes how responses should sound and what the agent should avoid.

## Training your agent

<Steps>
  <Step title="Gather source materials" icon="folder">

  Collect the documents, pages, and messaging assets your team already trusts. Prioritize current materials that explain your product, pricing, policies, and ideal customer conversations.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload or connect knowledge sources" icon="upload">

  Add your source materials to the platform using the supported upload or connection method. After the content is ingested, confirm that the latest versions of your key materials are available to the agent.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure brand voice and tone preferences" icon="settings">

  Set the tone you want the agent to use across conversations, such as formal, conversational, or technical. Add clear writing guidance so responses stay consistent across different question types and customer segments.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Define conversation guardrails and prohibited topics" icon="shield">

  Tell the agent what it should not say, promise, or discuss. Use guardrails to prevent unsupported claims, keep responses within policy, and route sensitive topics to a human teammate.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Test with sample conversations" icon="play-circle">

  Run realistic prompts based on questions your sales or support team receives every week. Check whether the agent answers correctly, follows your tone guidelines, and avoids restricted topics before you publish.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish the trained agent" icon="rocket">

  Publish only after the agent performs consistently across common and high-risk scenarios. Once live, monitor early conversations closely so you can catch gaps and improve training quickly.

  </Step>
</Steps>

## Brand voice

Brand voice controls how the agent delivers the information it knows. A **formal** tone works well for regulated or enterprise conversations, a **conversational** tone fits approachable sales and support interactions, and a **technical** tone helps when your audience expects precise product detail.

Set voice guidelines in clear, practical terms. Define preferred terminology, phrases to avoid, how direct the agent should be, and when it should keep answers short versus provide more detail.

<Callout kind="tip">

Review and update your training materials at least quarterly. Product changes, new FAQs, and shifted messaging all affect answer quality.

</Callout>

## Best practices

<Columns cols={2}>
  <Card title="Keep FAQs Concise" href="#" icon="help-circle" iconColor="#339cac">

  Write FAQ entries in direct language and answer one question at a time. Short, specific source content is easier for the agent to apply accurately.

  </Card>
  <Card title="Review Conversation Logs" href="#" icon="terminal" iconColor="#339cac">

  Inspect real conversations to find weak answers, missing context, and repeated fallback behavior. Use those patterns to decide what content to add or revise.

  </Card>
  <Card title="Update Quarterly" href="#" icon="refresh-cw" iconColor="#339cac">

  Revisit training on a regular schedule instead of waiting for visible failures. Small product and messaging changes accumulate quickly and reduce answer quality over time.

  </Card>
  <Card title="Test Before Publishing" href="#" icon="check-circle" iconColor="#339cac">

  Validate the agent against common questions, objection handling, and handoff scenarios before making it live. A short test cycle catches most training gaps early.

  </Card>
</Columns>
