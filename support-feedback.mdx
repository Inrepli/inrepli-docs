## Open the Inrepli Assistant

Signed-in users can open the Inrepli Assistant from the floating button on product pages. The Assistant keeps its conversation available as you move through the platform, so you can ask for help with the page and workflow you are using.

## Send Product Feedback

Tell the Assistant when you encounter a bug, want to request a feature, or have an improvement to suggest. Include what you were trying to do and the result you expected. The Assistant can capture the feedback together with the current page context and shows a confirmation card in the conversation.

The confirmation identifies the feedback type and summary so you can check that the request was understood correctly.

## Email Follow-up

When your Inrepli user has an email address, Inrepli can send an acknowledgement after the feedback is accepted for review. If the product team later marks a customer bug, feature request, or improvement as addressed, Inrepli can send a completion email with the relevant resolution link.

Questions and ordinary support conversations do not automatically become product feedback. Describe the bug, request, or improvement clearly when you want it recorded for product review.
